Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough, about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx). No online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals — and no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. Free roam stays open after the story for all cleanup except the one missable meditation.

    The 100% completion checklist (A Long Road)

    The checklist only appears after the story, under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion, and progress made during the story already counts. It asks for roughly 60% of true completion — not everything. Figures: PowerPyx / KeenGamer.

    WhatNeeded

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy in the whole list can be missed, according to PowerPyx, KeenGamer and GTABase. Every other trophy can be earned in free roam after the story.

    How to stay safe

    • Make a manual save at the beginning and end of every chapter (15 save slots). There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus.
    • Make a manual save before the final mission.
    • Leave cheats off: using a cheat disables trophies and saving.
    • Some optional missions become unavailable during certain story sections, but there are more optional tasks than the 100 required for completion, so missing a few will not block A Long Road.
